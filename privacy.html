Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 22, 2026

At GitHub Account Age Checker (gh-age.pages.dev), we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle information when you use our service.

1. Information We Collect

Usage Data: Like most websites, we collect standard log files. This includes IP addresses, browser types, Internet Service Providers (ISP), date/time stamps, and referring pages. This data is used for site maintenance and analytics.

GitHub Data: Our tool uses the public GitHub API to calculate account ages. We process this data in real-time. We do not store your GitHub username, email, or any other profile data on our servers.

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

We use cookies to enhance your experience and analyze site traffic. Third-party vendors, including Google and Adsterra, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites.

3. Third-Party Privacy Policies

Our Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. We advise you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information.

4. GDPR and CCPA Compliance

We respect your data rights. Since we do not store personal identification information, your right to access, rectify, or erase data is inherently protected. For any concerns, please contact us.
